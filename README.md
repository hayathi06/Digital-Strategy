# Digital-Strategy
Digital Strategy for Nexus Healthcare
Overview

This project focuses on developing a comprehensive digital strategy for Nexus Healthcare. The strategy aims to enhance the organization’s digital capabilities and improve healthcare services, specifically focusing on telehealth, AI innovations, and staff training. Using strategic analysis frameworks such as PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, and TOWS, the project provides a structured approach to transforming the organization's digital landscape.
Key Sections of the Report:

    Introduction: Provides background on Nexus Healthcare, the methodologies used, the scope of the project, and the structure of the report.

    Digital Strategic Analysis: Analyzes both the external and internal environment of Nexus Healthcare, using PESTLE, Porter’s Five Forces, SWOT, and value chain analysis to identify key challenges and opportunities.

    Defining Digital Strategic Objectives and Actions: Converts the SWOT analysis into actionable strategies and sets clear priorities for Nexus Healthcare, including high-priority objectives like strengthening telehealth services, implementing staff training programs, leveraging AI, and conducting risk assessments.

    Implementation Challenges: Discusses the challenges associated with the implementation of the digital strategy, such as technological complexity, staff training, cybersecurity risks, and budget constraints.

    Digital Strategy Evaluation: Evaluates the effectiveness of the proposed strategy using a balanced scorecard approach.
